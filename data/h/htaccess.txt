'''<dfn>.htaccess</dfn>''' is the file used for configuring particular sites on [[apache]] (as opposed to httpd.conf which is global).

== How to ==
{{section-stub}}
=== How to block POST ===
If you need to block all POST requests, e.g. due to an underlying language handling vulnerability (e.g. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PHP2Shell PHP2Shell]), you can add the following lines to your .htaccess:
<pre>
RewriteEngine on
RewriteCond %{REQUEST_METHOD} POST
RewriteRule .* - [F,L]
</pre>
