The '''<dfn>2020s</dfn>''' are a decade that began with a global pandemic, shutting down in-person events like [[IndieWebCamps]] and [[Homebrew Website Club]] meetups, inspiring growth of virtual meetups as a replacement, until broad vaccinations and dropping infection rates enabled cautious restarting of IndieWebCamps starting in [[2021/Düsseldorf|Düsseldorf]] in November 2021, then other European [[cities]], returning to the <abbr title="United States">US</abbr> ([[San Diego]]) in [[2023/SD|2023]].
