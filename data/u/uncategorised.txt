'''<dfn>uncategorised</dfn>''' is a page that lists [[posts]] on your website that do not have an assigned category.

== Why ==

* An /uncategorised page makes it easy for you to see posts you have published that do not have a category.

== IndieWeb Examples ==

* {{capjamesg}} has a /uncategorised page on his website that is always generated. If the page has no posts, a message will appear saying "There are no posts in this category.".
** Previously his [[SSG]] did not generate /uncategorised if there were no uncategorised posts. This meant the page would be out of sync.
** /uncategorized (American-English spelling) redirects to /uncategorised.
