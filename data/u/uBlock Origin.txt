'''<dfn>uBlock Origin</dfn>''' is an ad blocking browser plugin that allows users to subscribe to a selection of blocklists and/or create their own per-site element-filtering rules using a CSS-like selector syntax. Several Indieweb chat members use it to dynamically page sections they don’t want to see on sites like YouTube, LinkedIn, and GitHub. As of Sep. 2026, it is currently available for the Firefox, Brave, and Edge browsers. .
