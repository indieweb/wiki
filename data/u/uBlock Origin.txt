[https://ublockorigin.com/ '''<dfn>uBlock Origin</dfn>'''] is a free, open-source ad blocking browser plugin that allows users to subscribe to a selection of blocklists and/or create their own per-site element-filtering rules using a [[CSS]]-like selector syntax. Several Indieweb chat members use it to dynamically hide page sections they don’t want to see on sites like [[YouTube]], [[LinkedIn]], and [[GitHub]]. As of Sep. 2026, it is currently available for the desktop versions of [[Firefox]], [[Brave]], and [[Edge]] browsers.

== IndieWeb Examples ==
* {{lesleylai}} has a [https://gist.github.com/LesleyLai/ae381406628bcfef5a470b9542eeb1d9 gist] showing uBlock Origin rules to hide copilot from [[github]]
* [[user:Www.morganwebdev.org]] hides the [[LinkedIn]] news feed using the rule <code>www.linkedin.com##aside[aria-label='Aside']</code>.
*{{kupietz}} has a few short posts giving examples of things you can do with uBlock Origin rules: [https://michaelkupietz.com/a-ublock-origin-custom-rule-to-hide-related-videos-and-more-on-youtube/ hiding the related videos section on YouTube], [https://michaelkupietz.com/?p=31606 hiding Instagram share posts from the Facebook feed] which also includes a general how-to about uBlock Origin and creating filtering rules, and [https://michaelkupietz.com/filtering-linkedin-posts-notifications-and-comments-by-keyword/ Hiding LinkedIn posts, comments, and notifications based on Keywords] (last two are older; not confirmed working since 2025, test before relying on.)
