'''<dfn>IndieWebCamp San Diego 2026</dfn>''' is the fourth IndieWebCamp [[San Diego]] (AKA <dfn><abbr>IWC SD</abbr></dfn>) and planned for the weekend of December 12-13th, 2026. Questions may be directed to [[User:Artlung.com|Joe Crawford]] in [[discuss|indieweb chat]].

* '''When:''' 2026-12-12 and 2026-12-13, 10:00-18:00
* '''Where:''' Rio Vista Station, 92108, San Diego, California
* '''Wiki:''' https://indieweb.org/2026/SD
* '''Cost:''' tickets complimentary

=== Possible field trips ===
* [https://www.thevalleysd.com/ The Valley San Diego] mall, includes a Target, AMC Theaters, Comics N Stuff, and [https://www.thevalleysd.com/directory many other stores]
* [https://verbatimbooks.com/ Verbatim Books]
* High end theaters
** https://www.thelotent.com 
** https://www.cinepolisusa.com/
* Museums at Balboa Park
** There’s lots of small museums that are more like large galleries.
** Classy ones like Museum of Photographic Arts: https://balboapark.org/arts-culture/museum-of-photographic-arts-at-the-san-diego-museum-of-art/
** Quirky ones like the one for model railroads: https://www.sdmrm.org/
** And many more, too many to list.
* [https://sandiegoriver.org/discover/about-grant-park/ Grant Park]
** venue is right along the San Diego River and there are running/walking paths alongside with good walks, Grant Park is open on a limited basis
* [https://missionvalley.sdsu.edu/riverpark River Park SDSU]
** One trolley stop east, has walking paths, picnic tables, scenic
