== spam or misuse accounts ==
There are a small handful of Mastodon or Bluesky accounts that are either spamming the #indieweb hashtag, or using it for a post that happens to be posted on an indieweb site (and syndicated to Mastodon), but is not about anything actually indieweb related.

Until we have a mechanism to block these accounts from the stream hashtag feeds, we (as a community) must manually remove their spam from the chat archives on a daily basis.
* See: [[discuss_infrastructure#How-to_remove_spam]] for how to manually remove spam from our chat archives.
** #stream archive folder: https://github.com/indieweb/indieweb-chat-archive/tree/main/freenode/%23indieweb-stream

=== using indieweb hashtag on all POSSE posts ===
These profiles appear to put the #indieweb hashtag on every [[POSSE]] post from their personal site. While we strongly encourage POSSEing your posts to wherever your friends read them, there is no need to hashtag them #indieweb or #personalSite or related generic hashtag. Perhaps #POSSEpost to indicate that they are syndicated from your personal site.
* bsky.app/profile/did:plc:2dhkq3pctktbuo5dsylv2ddc
* mastodon.social/@lzon
* ...

If your profile is on this list above, feel free to join [[discuss|chat]] and ask to be unblocked, assuming you've stopped using the #indieweb hashtag on every [[POSSE]] post.

== FAQ ==
=== How does blog search work? ===
There is a manually managed list of blogs that are subscribed to in [[Aperture]]. The Aperture channel has some additional filtering to only show posts that include relevant keywords (listed in "blog search" above).

There has been some discussion about automating this more, perhaps from [[chat-names]], but it is still manual as of 2026-06-08.

* [https://chat.indieweb.org/meta/2024-02-15#t1707956226403000 2024 chat discussion about how indieweb sites are included]

=== Why is my Mastodon post not appearing? ===
The Mastodon RSS feeds mentioned above are aggregated into one feed which is then subscribed to in Aperture. The Aperture channel has some additional filtering to only show posts that include relevant keywords (listed in "blog search" above). In some instances of federating posts to Mastodon, the hashtag does not appear in the post content, so even if you used <code>#indieweb</code>, the text "indieweb" does not appear in the post and is excluded from the stream.

This is our understanding as of 2026-04-29 and may be updated in the future.
