'''<dfn>seeking</dfn>''' is a [[slash page]] (typically <code>/seeking</code> or <code>/seeking/</code>) on a personal website where someone describes things they are actively looking for and how others can help — people, places, objects, opportunities, collaboration, or reconnecting — without handing the request to a matching platform.

== Why ==
A seeking page keeps asks on your own domain: readable by humans first, portable via open formats, and contactable without a silo account. It is related to [[want]] posts, but as a maintained named page rather than only datestamped posts.

== How ==
Publish a readable seeking page with explicit wanted requests and a public way to respond. For a portable profile (Microformats2 <code>h-listing</code>, stable request identities, lifecycle, OPML, chronological feeds), see Seeking Pages Profile 1.0:

* [https://joshuamorris.info/seeking/docs/ /Seeking Standard]

See also: [[page]], [[Category:Slashpages]]
