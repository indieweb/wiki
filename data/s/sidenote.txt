'''<dfn>sidenotes</dfn>''' and '''<dfn>margin notes</dfn>''' are notes in the margin of a web page and are visually connected to the text to which they relate.

== Difference between sidenotes and margin notes ==

The two terms are sometimes used interchangeably, but some writers distinguish them <ref>[https://gwern.net/sidenote Sidenotes In Web Design]</ref>. A "sidenote" is numbered and anchored to a specific point in the text, much like a [[footnote|footnotes]]. A "margin note" is the unnumbered variant, with no fixed anchor.

== Why ==

Sidenotes (and margin notes) are useful to convey "aside" text or references related to the main content, but are not strictly necessary to read.

While footnotes remain the de facto standard for this kind of text, sidenotes do not require the reader to jump back and forth, which makes them ideal for websites.
