'''<dfn>sidenotes</dfn>''' and '''<dfn>margin notes</dfn>''' are notes in the margin of a web page and are visually connected to the text to which they relate.

== IndieWeb Examples ==

* {{tracydurnell}} uses sidenotes on her website.
** Documented how her sitenotes work at https://tracydurnell.com/2026/02/02/super-simple-sidenotes/.
* {{vhbelvadi}} uses sidenotes on his website.
** Example: https://vhbelvadi.com/footnotes-on-the-web
* {{lesleylai}} uses margin notes extensively on his blog posts.
** Example: https://lesleylai.info/en/fifty_shades_of_oop/
