'''<dfn>Send a friend a webmention day</dfn>''' started in 2023 as a day on which we encourage people to send a [[Webmention]] to a friend, coinciding with International Friendship Day on July 31st.

== IndieWeb Examples ==

=== Sara Jakša ===
{{sarajaksa}} wrote [https://sarajaksa.eu/2023/07/send-a-friend-a-webmention-day-or-some-of-my-thinking-about-webmentions/ a post] about webmentions trying to include as many people as possible and sent the webmentions to all that accepted it.

=== Pablo Morales ===
{{lifeofpablo}} wrote [https://lifeofpablo.com/blog/send-a-friend-a-webmention-day a send a friend] a webmention post to various people on the indieweb.
