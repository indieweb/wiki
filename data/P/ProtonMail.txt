'''<dfn>[https://protonmail.com ProtonMail]</dfn>''' is an [[email]] service hosted in Switzerland that supports end-to-end encryption and markets itself in terms of [[privacy]]. With a business account, you can use your own domain with the service. See https://proton.me/business
