'''<dfn>[https://joinplu.me Plume]</dfn>''' is an [[open source]] blogging tool based on [[ActivityPub]] and has some [[microformats2]] support. The project site notes that “developers have less time and Plume is not actively maintained” since at least [http://web.archive.org/web/20201221083420/https://joinplu.me/ 2020-12], though updates have occasionally been [https://github.com/Plume-org/Plume/tags released] through 2022 as of this writing.

* Open source: https://github.com/Plume-org/Plume

== Features ==
Plume users and blogs can be followed by other ActivityPub software, though the display will differ depending on the respective project's focus. [https://indieweb.org/pleroma Pleroma] displays the title, summary, and content, which may trigger its collapsed view for longer content. The post contains a link to the post's permalink and the author's profile page. In [[Mastodon]], the post summary is used as a CW with the title and permalink hidden under the fold. There is an open [https://github.com/tootsuite/mastodon/issues/8609 issue] to improve the display within Mastodon.

=== microformats ===
2018-12-08 added [[microformats2]] support: [https://github.com/Plume-org/Plume/issues/229 #229 Enhancement Proposal: Add Microformats Markup] and https://github.com/Plume-org/Plume/pull/341

There was a Plume at <nowiki>https://baptiste.gelez.xyz/~/FederationTesting/plume-and-the-indie-web post</nowiki> that demonstrated microformats support, but it is offline as of 2026 and does not appear in Internet Archive snapshots. Please update this section if another example is found.
