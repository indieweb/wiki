A '''<dfn>title</dfn>''' may refer to the contents of the HTML title tag on a web page, which may or may not be the same as the contents of a h1 on the page.

== How to ==

At [[events/2025-09-03-hwc-europe]], there was a discussion about how to structure document <code>title</code>. The general pattern was:

 [document title] [separator] [site name]

For example:

 Announcing rainfall.scot | James' Coffee Blog

Separators you can use include:

* | (pipe)
* - (dash)
