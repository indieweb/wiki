'''<dfn>Nitter</dfn>''' (<nowiki>nitter.net</nowiki>) was a free and open source alternative [[Twitter]] front-end focused on privacy, shut down in 2024 due to the Twitter API workarounds no longer working.

== Features ==
* [[RSS]] feeds for Twitter user accounts
* Alternative profile URLs for Twitter profiles, e.g. [https://web.archive.org/web/20200729232546/https://nitter.net/t archived example]

== Cease & Desist ==

A letter dated August 25th appeared on https://nitter.net/ ([https://web.archive.org/web/20260825180412/https://nitter.net/ archived]):

<blockquote>
On 24 August 2026 cease and desist letters have been sent by X Corp. demanding a permanent takedown of Nitter instances and the project's repository.

nitter.net is offline and development has stopped for the time being. I'm seeking legal advice and won't be commenting further on the specifics for now.

Thank you to everyone who used, hosted, packaged, donated and contributed to Nitter over the past seven years. 
</blockquote>
