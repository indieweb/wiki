== Community Effects ==

=== Use of AI bots ===

In 2026, AI bots have:

* Joined HWC (see [[events/2026-08-05-hwc-asia-pacific-incident]]).
** At the same event, the AI bot joined and broke an Etherpad.
** The same bot later joined the IndieWeb chat and, after discouragement from the community, left.
* Joined the [[indiewebring]]
** Noticed on 2026-09-27 by winther.sysctl.dk, bot removed by {{schmarty}}.
* Submitted posts to IndieNews
** Happened several times in September. Posts were removed.
* Submitted entries to the IndieWeb Carnival and IndieWeb Fiction Carnival
** Accepting submissions is at the discretion of the host. On at least two occasions AI entries were rejected.
* Sent a Webmention
** {{capjamesg}} and {{aaronpk}} have both received webmentions from sites operated by AI bots.
