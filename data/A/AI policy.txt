'''<dfn>AI policy</dfn>''' is a page (or section like on an [[about]] page or [[homepage]]) that describes the site owner’s policy about use of AI (typically [[LLM]]s or other GenAI) on their site, and sometimes an explicit part of a contribution policy to a community effort such as a [[blogging challenge]].

=== Brainstorming HWC policy ===

* {{capjamesg}}'s thoughts based on {{burgeonlab}}'s excellent example above:

<blockquote>We do not allow use of AI-based transcription, recording, and note-taking tools during Homebrew Website Club. This applies to both the Zoom and Etherpad. If you breach this policy, we will ask you to leave the session, and we will inform other event organisers of the incident. If you would like or require closed captioning, please let the event organiser know before the call or when you join so we can make sure this is set up in Zoom for you.</blockquote>

* James would use such a policy on both HWC event listings on events.indieweb.org, and also call it out any time a new participant joins a meeting.
