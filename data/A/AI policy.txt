'''<dfn>AI policy</dfn>''' is a page (or section like on an [[about]] page or [[homepage]]) that describes the site owner’s policy about use of AI (typically [[LLM]]s or other GenAI) on their site, and sometimes an explicit part of a contribution policy to a community effort such as a [[blogging challenge]].

== IndieWeb Examples ==
AI policies on personal websites are typically posted at <kbd>/ai-policy</kbd> or <kbd>/ai</kbd>, sometimes part of an about page. 
* https://reillyspitzfaden.com/ai-policy/
* https://michaelkupietz.com/ai-policy/
* https://gregorlove.com/about/#ai (also [https://gregorlove.com/ai gregorlove.com/ai] redirects there)
* {{artlung}} added his own [https://artlung.com/ai/ /ai] [[slash page]] in late 2025.
* https://brennan.day/ai/

in general:
