'''<dfn>307</dfn>''' is 307 Temporary Redirect, and should be used instead of [[302]] because it requires preserving the original request method (in contrast to how 302 was historically implemented), like a POST request MUST be resent as a POST request in the future, like for [[Webmention]] endpoints that are temporarily elsewhere.
