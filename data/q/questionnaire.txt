A '''<dfn>questionnaire</dfn>''' is a list of questions that you may answer on your personal website. It's a common kind of blogging [[challenge]], sometimes called a "questions challenge".

== How it works ==
Someone publishes a set of questions, usually with their own answers, and invites others to answer too. Each response typically:
* links back to whoever started the questionnaire, and often to the post that introduced the author to it
* invites readers with a personal site to answer as well
* optionally sends a [[Webmention]] or otherwise notifies the original author, who may collect responses
