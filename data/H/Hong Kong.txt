== Homebrew Website Club ==

=== Online Events ===

Fortnightly [https://events.indieweb.org/tag/hwc HWC] events are hosted online, over Zoom. The two held in the Americas are "convenient" in Hong Kong Time (UTC +8): 

* HWC: US Eastern at 06:00 HKT (07:00 DST)
** Venue: [https://events.indieweb.org/tag/eastern Zoom link] will be shown 15 minutes before the event
* HWC: US Pacific at 09:00 HKT (10:00 DST)
** Venue: [https://events.indieweb.org/tag/pacific Zoom link] will be shown 15 minutes before the event

On August 5,2026 {{burgeonlab}} started a new event dedicated to the South East Asia / Pacific / Oceania time zones.

* HWC: Asia Pacific at 17:30 HKT
** Venue: Online, see the Event page [https://events.indieweb.org/tag/asiapacific HWC: Asia Pacific Edition].

=== In-Person Events ===

There are no plans for one yet; but if there is [[Homebrew_Website_Club#New_Cities|enough interest]] in Hong Kong, {{burgeonlab}} is happy to help organize something offline.
