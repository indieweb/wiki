'''<dfn>[https://codepen.io CodePen]</dfn>''' is a [[silo]] for hosting [[HTML]], [[CSS]] and [[JavaScript]] snippets to demonstrate the capabilities of the client-side Web.

== Features ==
* Profile
** [[rel-me]] linking to multiple other sites! E.g. https://codepen.io/adactio
* [[Export]]
** CodePen pens can be exported to [[ZIP]] files or to [[GitHub]] gists.
* Premium accounts can save private pens
* [[API]]
** CodePen's [https://blog.codepen.io/docs/api/api-v2/ API v2]
<blockquote>''The CodePen API v2 lets you read and write your CodePen Pens, Collections, and Assets from your own tools: scripts, build steps, CI jobs, editors, and AI agents.''</blockquote>
** ''[https://www.raymondcamden.com/2026/10/06/adding-my-latest-codepens-to-my-blog Adding My Latest CodePens to My Blog]'' by Raymond Camden (uses [[JavaScript]] to capture pens to an [[SSG]])
