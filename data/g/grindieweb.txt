'''''The Grand Rapids IndieWeb Group is in its formative stages.'''''

Independent web enthusiasts focused on building a better web by publishing content for humans by humans.

No technical expertise required. Just a passion for writing/blogging on the web.

We meet monthly to share our work, encourage others, and develop/learn new skills. New bloggers of any experience welcome!

"We are a community of independent and personal websites based on the principles of: owning your domain and using it as your primary online identity, publishing on your own site first (optionally elsewhere), and owning your content."<ref>[https://indieweb.org]</ref>
