'''<dfn>[https://powerautomate.microsoft.com/ Microsoft Power Automate]</dfn>''' (formerly <dfn>Microsoft Flow</dfn>) is an alternative to [[IFTTT]] for connecting apps and services with automation. It was originally free, but as of at least 2026, they only offer a 30-day free trial. [https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/products/power-automate/pricing]
