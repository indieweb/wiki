'''<dfn>concerts</dfn>''' is a top level [[page]] on some [[personal site|personal sites]] that lists the [[music]] and often other performance [[event]]s someone has been to, sometimes with ticket stubs or other related materials.

== IndieWeb Examples ==
Roughly ordered by earliest content.

=== Lynn Fisher ===
Lynn has posted the concerts they have been to since 1997.
* https://concerts.lynnandtonic.com/

=== Jakob ===
Jakob posts the concerts he has been to since 2025 his past events page which he built and [[2026/Düsseldorf/Demos#Jakob|demonstrated at IndieWebCamp DUS 2026]]
* https://events.bollenz.de/past-events.php
He also lists concerts he is going to or wants to on his events page:
* https://events.bollenz.de/

=== Joe ===
{{artlung}} first [[blog|blogged]] a list of concerts in 2014, and created a dedicated [[slashpage]] in 2026: [https://artlung.com/concerts/ artlung.com/concerts/]

=== gRegor ===
{{gRegor}} lists concerts on the [[events]] page https://gregorlove.com/events/past
* Other types of events (plays, musicals, meetups, etc.) are included, but the majority are concerts
* Concerts are also tagged with #concert: https://gregorlove.com/tags/concert/

=== Thamara Kandabada ===
{{thamarakandabada}} has a [https://thamara.co.uk/gigs gigs] page where he lists live performances he's attended. His definition of "gig" in the context of this page is quite broad—it includes music, theatre, comedy, and many other kinds of performance art.

These updates are marked up with [[h-feed]] and are also available via an [[RSS]] feed.
