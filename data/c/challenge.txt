A '''<dfn>challenge</dfn>''' on the indieweb usually means one of two things:
* a '''blogging challenge''': a topic to write about, or a list of questions to answer, in a single post, often passed along or "tagged" by others
* an '''ongoing challenge''': an event-like invitation to post progress towards a goal over a set period, like [[exercise]] or [[photo challenge|posting photos at some frequency]] (e.g. daily), also supported by silos like [[Strava]] and [[micro.blog]]

== Kinds of Challenges ==

=== Questionnaires ===
{{main|questionnaire}}

A list of questions you answer in a post on your own site, then invite others to answer too. Responses often link back to whoever started the questionnaire, or to whoever passed it on. See [[questionnaire]] for the full list with example responses, including:
* [[questionnaire#Blog_Questions_Challenge|Blog Questions Challenge]] (2025)
* [[questionnaire#Proust_Questionnaire|Proust Questionnaire]]
* [[questionnaire#Sandwich_Questionnaire|Sandwich Questionnaire]]
* [[questionnaire#Food_Questions_Challenge|Food Questions Challenge]]
* [[questionnaire#Cooking_Conundrums_Challenge|Cooking Conundrums Challenge]]

=== Prompts and Carnivals ===
A single theme or prompt, often with a host who collects responses into a roundup.
* [[blog carnival]]
** [[IndieWeb Carnival]]
** [[IndieWeb Fiction Carnival]]
** [[IndieWeb Book Club]]
** [[IndieWeb Movie Club]]

=== Collaborative Challenges ===
Challenges where two or more people write in response to each other.
* Title trades: two people swap blog post titles, and each writes a post for the title they received. The idea came from [https://kami.bearblog.dev/lets-trade-blogposts/ Kami]. {{zachary.kai}} keeps a directory of people willing to trade at [https://zacharykai.net/lists/titletrade Title Traders].

=== Ongoing and Time-Based Challenges ===
Challenges that involve multiple posts, usually at a regular frequency like daily, for a set period such as a week, a month, or [[100 days]].

==== Daily ====
* [https://daily.ds106.us/ The Daily Create]: <blockquote>“Each day you get a new creative challenge in photo, drawing, audio, video, or writing form, that you can do in 20 minutes or less. Every day since January 8, 2012.”</blockquote>
