'''<dfn>Wrong on the internet</dfn>''' is the last phrase of [https://xkcd.com/386/ XKCD #386], “Duty Calls”, a satirical comic expressing the difficulty of not arguing with someone making a mistaken statement on the internet, often on [[social media]], however also on [[blog]]s or other independent websites.

== 386ing ==
<dfn>386ing</dfn> is the act of spending lots of time looking into how people are wrong on the internet and/or posting corrections on the internet accordingly, in reference to the behavior of the person shown in the “Duty Calls” comic.

Appears to have been originally [https://slatestarcodex.com/2015/05/22/ai-researchers-on-ai-risk/#comment-205409 coined on 2015-05-22 by '''Stephen Frug''' in a comment] on <cite>[https://slatestarcodex.com/2015/05/22/ai-researchers-on-ai-risk/ AI Researchers On AI Risk]</cite> wherein he accurately uses it to label the very behavior he is engaging in: <blockquote>Which is why I’m spending my Friday night XKCD 386ing with you here in the comments. Which, frankly, I should probably stop.</blockquote>
