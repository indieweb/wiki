'''<dfn>web weaving</dfn>''' is the activity of working on your website, which may include writing, (re-)designing, coding, organising, planning, [[backfilling]], and more.

== History ==

* Used in the [[32-Bit Cafe]] community.
<blockquote>I adopted the term "webweaving" from 32-Bit Cafe to refer to the activity of making and developing websites in hobby contexts. </blockquote> (https://leilukin.com/links/webweaving/)
** {{capjamesg}} first heard the term used in the 32-Bit Cafe.
