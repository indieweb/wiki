'''Four circle Venn diagrams'''

From https://www.google.com/search?q=examples+of+four+circle+venn+diagrams:

[[Image:2026-08-23-google-gemini-four-circle-venn-error.png]]

None of those images are proper Venn diagrams, nor does the description accurately describe Venn diagrams in particular.

The diagrams are Euler diagrams, missing two of the possible relationships between the different sets.

The description fails to note the key aspect of a Venn diagram, that it displays/demonstrates ALL relationships between different sets in the diagram.

This appears to be a case of <abbr>GIGO</abbr> (garbage in, garbage out) of the pop misunderstanding of Venn diagrams being more everpresent on the web (presumably its training data) than the proper understanding of it, so Gemini is merely reflecting a pop misunderstanding, though with the confidence of being supposedly accurate.

See [[Wikipedia:Venn diagram]] for more details on both of those.

== Gemini protocol ==
{{main|Gemini protocol}}

The gemini protocol allows users with dedicated clients (such as [https://gmi.skyjake.fi/lagrange/ Lagrange]) download documents and explore following links in a similar fashion to the regular web. It is a modern protocol but with a focus in simplicity and security.

The associated file format to this protocol is the gemtext which displays only Text lines, Link lines, Heading lines, List items, Quote lines and Preformat toggle lines.
