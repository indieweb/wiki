'''<dfn>[https://ko-fi.com/ Ko-fi]</dfn>''' is a service to send [[payment]]s to support content creators financially as a [[paid subscription]] or one payment at a time. One of their default [[share]] buttons has the text "Buy me a [[coffee]]."

== Features ==
* payments are processed through [[PayPal]] or [[Stripe]] directly to the creator.
* currently no fees to use Ko-fi, but PayPal/Stripe fees likely apply.
