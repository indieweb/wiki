'''<dfn>reading pages</dfn>''' are a personal-site [[slash page]] collection (typically <code>/reading/</code>) of books and reading status — queued, currently reading, finished, reviewed — with stable book URLs. They complement individual [[read]] posts and often overlap with a [[personal library]].

== Why ==
A reading collection keeps shelves, history, and discovery on your own domain instead of a commercial reading silo. Feeds and markup make the collection usable by other personal sites.

== How ==
Publish a reading index and per-book pages with clear status. For a reusable profile (book identity, history, Microformats2, OPML, ratings contract), see Reading Pages Profile 1.0:

* [https://joshuamorris.info/reading/docs/ /Reading Standard]

See also: [[personal library]], [[read]], [[IndieWeb Book Club]] (monthly carnival — complementary social layer)
