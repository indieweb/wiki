Using '''<dfn>[https://microformats.org/wiki/rel-tag rel=tag]</dfn>''' on a hyperlink indicates that its destination represents a [[tag]] for the current page, is used by some [[CMS]]s, and was indexed by search engines like [[Technorati]] in the past.

[[posts]] should use <code>p-category</code> or <code>u-category</code> properties inside their [[h-entry]] to tag their posts.
* See the [[tags]] page for details on how to.

See the [https://microformats.org/wiki/rel-me rel-tag specification] for more details on how to publish.
