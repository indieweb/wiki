'''<dfn>Blaugust</dfn>''' is an annual community-led [[blogging challenge]], labeled a “festival of blogging”, to post 31 times during the month of [[August]] (optionally with hashtag #blaugust) since 2014, most recently described in “[https://nerdgirlthoughts.game.blog/2026/07/15/blaugust-2026-is-coming/ Blaugust 2026 is Coming!]”, with awards & badges you can earn by participating.

== History ==
Blaugust was started in 2014 by [https://aggronaut.com/ Belghast], and has taken place every year since except 2017. In 2020 the challenge took place in April and was called "Blapril".
