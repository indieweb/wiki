Priče iz Bosne (bosnianux.com) is a cultural portal dedicated to preserving Bosnian traditions, legends, and history through storytelling.
IndieWeb features:
Webmention: Supported for comments and reactions.
IndieAuth: Used for secure identity verification.
Microformats: The site uses h-entry for articles.
Technical Stack:
WordPress
Matomo Analytics (Self-hosted, cookieless)
