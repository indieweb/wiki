'''<dfn>December</dfn>''' is a month in the Gregorian calendar, the month of the collective community [[IndieWeb Gift Calendar]], and an excellent time of the year to [[planning|plan]] and [[IndieWebCamp Organizing|organize]] an [[IndieWebCamp]] in a warmer city, such as [[San Diego]] or [[Los Angeles]], or possibly [[San Francisco]].
