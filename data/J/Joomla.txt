'''<dfn>[https://joomla.org Joomla]</dfn>''' is a [[content management system]] that can be IndieWebified.
In 2026 Joomla celebrated its 21st birthday as an open-source CMS and still has an active community maintaining it.

== Criticism ==
* I have worked with several Joomla installations over the years (all in 1.x and 1.5.x versions) and found it to be a pain in the neck. The terminology and structure of the code in its MVC is confusing and relatively simple tasks have taken longer than necessary. I have dealt with several security problems and hacked installations — including a site that was being used as part of a botnet. Perhaps the core code is no less secure than other CMS (bugs happen), but I feel like there's a disproportionate amount of headaches for Joomla, perhaps because it encourages poor server practices. I won't pretend to be unbiased, here. I loathe Joomla and strongly recommend not using it. {{gRegor}}

* Adding my counter-opinion. I have worked with Joomla since v1.5.x and restarted creating sites with version 3.5.x. Since this time Joomla has evolved, and at time of writing (2026/09) is at 6.1.2. Much work has been made by the core team to revamp and modernise Joomla, remove legacy code and make it a lot easier and modular for developers. I find it to be a great CMS with the right plugins to help on the aesthetics for templating. I also won't pretend to be biased in the other direction but I think in its current state it has a lot going for it. [[User:Aftertaf.mont-gargan.fr|aftertaf]]
