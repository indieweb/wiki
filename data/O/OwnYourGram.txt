'''<dfn>OwnYourGram</dfn>''' was a service which streamed your [[Instagram]] [[photo]]s to your own [[site]] in real-time. After [[IndieAuth]] sign-in, it sent your Instagram photos to your [[Micropub]] endpoint.

* [https://github.com/aaronpk/OwnYourGram Original source] and [https://github.com/aaronpk/OwnYourGram/issues issue tracker]  on [[GitHub]]

== Features ==
=== Person Tagging ===
OwnYourGram detected when an Instagram photo had [[person-tag]]s, and then passed those along in the Micropub request.

* E.g. [https://aaronparecki.com/2015/08/19/18/geoloqi-kittenselfie aaronparecki.com/2015/08/19/18/geoloqi-kittenselfie]
* Implemented [https://github.com/aaronpk/OwnYourGram/commit/b688b521755438dbf1755273f5e157ff7d115681 2015-07-16] (requested [https://indiewebcamp.com/wiki/index.php?title=OwnYourGram&action=historysubmit&diff=20661&oldid=9295 2015-07-14][http://indiewebcamp.com/irc/2015-07-14#t1436900516486]!)

How:
* Detects IG @-username person-tags on a photo
* Translates those to personal site URLs from the person's Instagram profile
** If there is no such personal site URL, it sends the URL of the person's Instagram profile

The feature helped turn [[Instagram]] into an IndieWeb client capable of supporting Player A's client needs in [[SWAT0]].[http://indiewebcamp.com/irc/2015-07-14#t1436900498172]
