'''<dfn>[https://organicmaps.app/ Organic Maps]</dfn>''' is an offline first mapping and navigating mobile native app that works by downloading [[OpenStreetMap]] data for wherever you open it, and does not share and track your location with any servers, unlike popular silo mobile mapping apps like [[Google Maps]].
