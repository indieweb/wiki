'''<dfn>OutPost</dfn>''' is a [[Micropub]] client for [[iOS]] and [[MacOS]]. OutPost publishes notes and articles straight to your own website, signing in with [[IndieAuth]]. Your posts live on [[your domain]], not on someone else’s platform.

* https://jimmitchell.dev/outpost/

Developed by Jim Mitchell. Announcement blog post:
* https://jimmitchell.org/2026/09/22/outpost-for-mac-public-beta-available/
