Domain flipping is the act of purchasing a [[domain]] from a user and reselling it to other people (preferably buisnesses) at a marked up cost.

If you end up purchasing a domain, you might receive [[spam|unsolicited e-mails and messages]] from domain flippers trying to buy your domain off you. Not all domain flipping offers are scam offers automatically, but it is important to exercise caution.
