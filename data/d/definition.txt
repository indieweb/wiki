A '''<dfn>definition</dfn>''' is a post or section of a page quoting a new or interesting dictionary definition, often included in a [[glossary]] or [[digest|digests]] such as [[weeknotes]].

== Why ==

Definitions are a fun way to share what you are learning or clarify what you are communicating about.
