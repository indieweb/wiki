A '''<dfn>definition</dfn>''' is a post or section of a page quoting a new or interesting dictionary definition, often included in a [[glossary]] or [[digest|digests]] such as [[weeknotes]].

== Why ==

Definitions are a fun way to share interesting words you are learning or came across. In the context of a [[glossary]] definitions can clarify what you are communicating about, differentiate usage from other meanings of the word, and help readers understand you better.
