'''<dfn>Demos</dfn>''' are IndieWeb [[projects]] with instructions on how to demonstrate them, preferably quickly like under a minute or so, and lists of demo sessions from past [[IndieWebCamps]].

Demos may also happen as part of a [[Homebrew Website Club]]. For example, the [[HWC Europe]] meetup regularly calls on participants to demo new features on their websites.
