'''<dfn>print-on-demand</dfn>''’ describes services which turn digital files such as [[ebook|ebooks]], [[logos]], or [[photos]] into physical [[books]]. [[stickers]], and [[print|prints]]. Some services act as [[silos]] with [[account|user accounts]] where one can [[post]] files for others to [[purchase]], typically with revenue share minus cost of printing.


== Examples ==
{{section-stub}}
=== Photos ===
* [https://www.shutterfly.com Shutterfly]
* [https://www.snapfish.com/ Snapfish]

=== Books and Printed Matter ===
* [[Lulu]]

=== Business Cards ===
* [https://www.moo.com/ Moo] for business cards

=== Multiple ===
* [https://www.printful.com Printful.com]
* [https://www.redbubble.com/ Redbubble]



== IndieWeb Examples ==
{{section stub}}
* [[IndieWeb]] uses [https://www.printful.com Printful.com] for its [[swag]] available at [https://store.indieweb.org/ store.indieweb.org] (which redirects to https://indieweb.printful.me/)

* {{artlung}} has has a Redbubble account for many years to allow readers to purchase stickers, prints and t-shirts over the years.
* {{addyourself}}

== Past Examples ==
{{section-stub}}
* [[Photo]] [[silo]] [[Flickr]] at one time had a service called [https://www.flickr.com/groups/printboldly Print Boldly] for print on demand. Earlier in its history it had mechanisms to print directly from the site to external services such as Moo.com.
