Hey, I'm Arthur and I make sites and write on these sites. I'm from Porto Alegre, and usually blog on [https://irrelefante.com.br irrelefante.com.br].

== Tech Stack ==

My [https://arthr.me/ website] is in plain old HTML+CSS. My [https://irrelefante.com.br/ blog] uses [[Jekyll]] with [https://github.com/aarongustafson/jekyll-webmention_io jekyll-webmention_io] to add [[webmentions]] and [[POSSE]]. I plan to make the blog theme available for use soon.

I post [[notes]], [[articles]] and [https://microformats.org/wiki/h-review reviews]. I also share links I [[likes|like]]. There are separate RSS feeds for each post type available as well.

I try to auto-[[POSSE]] as much as possible using [[Bridgy]], but I still haven't figure it out a way for that to work 100% of the time. Maybe soon!
