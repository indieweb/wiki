== My IndieWeb Journey ==
I discovered the IndieWeb in December 2025 after discovering [https://omg.lol omg.lol] and writing about it. I decided to get serious about building my own corner of the independent web in January 2026, purchasing brennan.day and building it from scratch with [[Eleventy]].

Since then, I've:
* Built IndieWeb implementations including [[IndieAuth]] comments and [[Micropub]] support
* Created open-source IndieWeb-themed projects and themes
* Joined multiple [[webring]]s and built an extensive [[blogroll]]
* Participated in communities like [https://tilde.town Tilde.town] and omg.lol

== Values & Principles ==
My work is guided by principles documented at https://brennan.day/values/.

=== Applied Values ===
* '''Own your content''': Build for portability, not platform lock-in
* '''Accessibility first''': Everyone deserves access
* '''[[privacy|Privacy by default]]''': No tracking, no surveillance
* '''Performance matters''': Respect people's time and bandwidth
* '''Open source when possible''': Share knowledge, improve together
* '''Write in public''': Document the process, not just results
* '''Center marginalized voices''': Practice meaningful, intentional listening and deliberate amplification
* '''Mentorship without gatekeeping''': Lift others up and break down barriers to entry
* '''Sustainable over scalable''': Work for decades, not 10x now and burn out

=== My IndieWeb Principles ===
I've articulated my own principles for the IndieWeb movement:

* '''Good faith code. Good faith writing.''': The IndieWeb should reject invasive trackers, annoying advertisements, bloated webpages, and [[accessibility|a11y]]-hostile design. We should write from a place of good faith, believing the Internet can still be a tool for empowerment and creativity.
* '''A Pro-social Attitude''': The web is meant to be social. Sites should have some social element, even if as simple as basic contact information, enabling dialogue rather than one-way broadcasting.
* '''Be Fun. Be Accessible. Be Small.''': Sites can have personality and express creativity while remaining [[accessibility]] ([[WCAG]] compliant, screen reader friendly) and performant (compressed images, lazy loading, progressive enhancement, no-JavaScript fallback).

See [https://brennan.day/posts/2026-02-14-what-is-the-indieweb/ What is the IndieWeb?] for more information.

== Disability & Accessibility ==

I try to ensure all my web developement has accessibility as a core principle, including:

* Semantic HTML with proper heading structures and landmark elements
* Full keyboard navigation for all interactive elements
* Colour contrast meeting WCAG 2.1 AA standards
* Responsive design across all devices and screen sizes
* Performance focus for users on slower connections and assistive technology
* Plain language writing and meaningful image descriptions

See [https://brennan.day/accessibility/ /accessibility] for details.

=== Advocacy ===
* '''Mentorship without gatekeeping''': actively supporting newcomers, offering free technical help
* '''Pro bono work''': providing web development for community organizations through Berry House
* '''Mutual aid''': participating in and promoting mutual aid networks
* '''Harm reduction approach''': Recognizing that gradual transitioning to the IndieWeb is the most sustainable solution for people dependent on corporate platforms
