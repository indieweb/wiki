== Intro ==
Hello, I'm Thamara, furniture-peddler by day and wearer-of-many-hats by night. Currently based in Belfast, Northern Ireland. I've had a personal website since 2014.

I recently discovered the IndieWeb community, and I've been enjoying modifying my site to to adhere to the IndieWeb ethos. I'm usually active on the [https://indieweb.org/discuss IndieWeb chat] channels.

== Personal site ==
I recently migrated my site from [[WordPress]] to [[Astro]]. A static HTML version of the WordPress site is archived at [https://archive.thamara.co.uk/ archive.thamara.co.uk].

I wrote the Astro site by hand from the ground up without using any pre-packaged themes. It is still very much a work in progress. I have detailed my setup in the [https://thamara.co.uk/colophon colophon] and [https://thamara.co.uk/style style] pages.

All my content such as [https://thamara.co.uk/notebook blogposts], [https://thamara.co.uk/gigs gigs], and my [https://thamara.co.uk/desk desk setup updates] are posted on my site. At the time of this writing I do not post on any silos.
