'''Ben Meadows''' is a builder in Phoenix, Arizona.

His personal site [https://thebenmeadows.com thebenmeadows.com] is hand-written
static HTML with no framework, no analytics, no cookies and no third-party
requests. A cold load of the home page is under 100&nbsp;KB uncompressed.

== IndieWeb building blocks ==

* [[h-card]] on the home page
* [[rel-me]] links to GitHub, X, Bluesky and Nostr
* [[WebFinger]] at <code>/.well-known/webfinger</code>, reserving
  <code>acct:ben@thebenmeadows.com</code>
* [https://github.com/nostr-protocol/nips/blob/master/05.md NIP-05] and an [https://github.com/lnurl/luds LNURL] Lightning Address under
  <code>/.well-known/</code>
* Atom and RSS feeds, announced over [[WebSub]]

== Mirrors ==

The same build is served over Tor, [[I2P]], [[IPFS]], Arweave, Nostr,
[[Gemini]], gopher, and over SSH, telnet and anonymous FTP as a text edition.
The source is mirrored to Codeberg, Gitea, GitLab, SourceHut, Radicle, Tangled
and Software Heritage alongside GitHub.

Every build publishes a manifest of SHA-256 hashes for every file it ships, so a
visitor can verify that the copy they received is the copy that was published.
The manifest's own hash is signed to Nostr and timestamped into Bitcoin with
[https://opentimestamps.org OpenTimestamps].
