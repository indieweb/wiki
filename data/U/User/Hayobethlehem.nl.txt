I have published on the web since 1996 and on this domain since February 2001, through fifteen redesigns and one long detour through [[WordPress]]. The current site is hand-coded: no framework, no build step, no third-party JavaScript. By day I advise on digital matters in the Dutch government.

== Topics ==
Web stuff, communication, photography, book reviews, gear reviews.

== My site ==
'''https://hayobethlehem.nl''' is where everything goes first.
* '''Articles''' - long-form posts, going back to 2004
* '''Library''' - every book I read, as a database application with its own taxonomy, built out of reviews I salvaged from [[Goodreads]]
* '''Forge''' - things I am building: a photo gallery, a local news aggregator for Wassenaar with propaganda and misinformation filtering, and a searchable index for airsoft gear
* '''About''' - [[colophon]], a [[uses]] page, a [[blogroll]] and a wishlist

=== How it is built ===
* Vanilla PHP 8.5. No framework, no package manager, no dependencies.
* MariaDB, reached only through prepared statements.
* Shared [[web hosting]] at Hetzner, which keeps the code honest about what it costs to run.
* Source, build and deploy at Codeberg.

* No cookies, no analytics, no third-party JavaScript. All on purpose.

== What my site speaks ==
Implemented:
* [[Webmention]] - sending and receiving, hand-written, see below
* [[Atom]] - five feeds, no [[RSS]], on purpose
* [[microformats2]] - [[h-card]] on the front page, [[h-feed]] and [[h-entry]] on the changelog
* schema.org microdata on every article
* [[WebFinger]] - the domain answers for the name, so I am reachable as @hayo@hayobethlehem.nl
* [[rel-me]] - linked both ways, to [[Mastodon]] and Codeberg
* [[IndieAuth]] - my own authorization endpoint, so I can sign in elsewhere as my domain
* security.txt, signed, and a published OpenPGP key
* noai and noimageai, asking the scrapers to leave it be

Deliberately not implemented, and why:
* [[Micropub]] - posting happens in my own backend
* [[Microsub]] - no reader lives here
* [[WebSub]] - the feeds are polled, not pushed
* [[ActivityPub]] - my fediverse presence is an account elsewhere, and the WordPress plugin I tried was never going to be complete

=== Webmention setup ===
Endpoint: https://hayobethlehem.nl/webmention, advertised in the head of every page.
* Nothing is fetched at the moment you send. Verification happens later, from cron.
* I fetch the source, confirm it really links back, and then read it myself. Nothing is published automatically.
* Approved replies join the Mastodon conversation at the foot of the article; likes and reposts are named and shown with their authors rather than summed up as a count.
* Outgoing mentions go out on publish, to everything an article links to that has somewhere to be told.
