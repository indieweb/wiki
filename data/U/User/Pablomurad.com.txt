== About ==

'''Pablo Murad''', also known online as '''runawaydevil''', is a Brazilian self-taught programmer, Unix enthusiast, and supporter of the [[IndieWeb]], hacker culture, and independent online communities.

Born in 1987, Pablo first got online in 1995, when discovering new websites often involved computer magazines, recommendations from friends, and a fair amount of curiosity. It was a smaller, more personal web, filled with handmade pages, experimentation, and communities built around shared interests. Some of the friendships he made during those early years have lasted for decades.

He wrote his first programs in 1997 using Delphi 3, with a compiler that came with a computer magazine. In 1999, he discovered Python, and he has been learning and experimenting with computers ever since.

Pablo has a particular passion for '''Unix machines, command-line environments, shared shells, [[pubnix]]es, and [[tilde]] communities'''. He enjoys exploring operating systems, experimenting with alternative protocols, and building small, independent places on the internet.

His academic background includes degrees in law and psychoanalytic theory and clinical practice from Unifenas, as well as postgraduate business studies. He works in education and entrepreneurship through Portal IDEA and Grupo Murad.

He lives in São Lourenço, Minas Gerais, Brazil.

== Philosophy ==

Pablo believes that '''education is a fundamental right''' and that learning is a lifelong necessity.

To him, the internet is one of the most important tools ever created for making knowledge accessible, connecting people, and encouraging curiosity. He believes everyone should have the opportunity to learn, regardless of their background or circumstances.

His relationship with technology is driven by curiosity, experimentation, and the desire to understand how things work. He believes that sharing knowledge is just as important as acquiring it.

Pablo is a strong admirer of [[POSSE]] (Publish on your Own Site, Syndicate Elsewhere), digital autonomy, and the idea that people should have control over their own content and online identities.

He values a personal, decentralized, and diverse web, where independent websites, small communities, and experimental projects can coexist with larger platforms.

For him, the internet should be more than a collection of services to consume. It should also be a place to build, explore, teach, and learn.

== Projects and experiments ==

Pablo maintains several independent projects, many of them related to Unix culture, alternative internet technologies, personal publishing, and knowledge sharing.

* '''[https://runv.club runv.club]''' — A shared Unix shell community dedicated to learning, experimentation, and connecting people who enjoy exploring computing.

* '''[https://noctem.cafe noctem.cafe]''' — An OpenBSD-based pubnix project, currently under construction.

* '''[https://noctem9.cafe noctem9.cafe]''' — An experimental shared computing space running 9front, a modern fork of Bell Labs' Plan 9. Members can explore a radically different approach to computing through drawterm, the rio window system, and personal tilde pages. Still under construction.

* '''[https://ascii.zone ascii.zone]''' — An independent project exploring ASCII/ANSI art, digital culture, and alternative internet protocols such as Gopher and Gemini.

* '''[https://bimb.ooo bimb.ooo]''' — A small independent publishing experiment for text, snippets, Markdown documents, and simple HTML pages.

Most of these projects begin with curiosity, the desire to learn something new, or simply the pleasure of building things for the internet.

Some are useful tools, others are ongoing experiments, and some exist just because making something interesting is reason enough.

== Computing interests ==

Pablo's technical interests include:

* Unix, BSD, and Linux operating systems
* Shared shells, pubnixes, and tilde communities
* Self-hosting and independent infrastructure
* Command-line tools and text-based interfaces
* Free and open-source software
* Computer history and hacker culture
* Decentralized networks and independent communities
* Personal publishing and alternative web technologies

He is particularly fascinated by the culture surrounding shared Unix systems and the idea of computers as spaces where people can meet, experiment, and learn from one another.

== IndieWeb and protocols ==

Pablo is interested in technologies that allow people to maintain independent digital identities, publish content on their own websites, and communicate across different networks.

His interests include:

* [[POSSE]]
* [[IndieAuth]]
* [[Webmention]]
* [[ActivityPub]]
* AT Protocol
* Nostr
* Gopher
* Gemini
* [[RSS]]

He enjoys exploring how modern decentralized technologies relate to older internet protocols and communities.

He is especially interested in approaches that encourage independent publishing, interoperability, and the long-term preservation of digital knowledge.

== Currently working on ==

Among his ongoing experiments are the development of '''noctem.cafe''' and '''noctem9.cafe''', both intended as independent spaces inspired by Unix and shared computing culture.

He also continues to maintain and improve his personal web projects while exploring independent publishing tools, alternative protocols, and ways to make technology more accessible to curious people.

== Beyond computers ==

Outside computing, Pablo enjoys photography, literature, cinema, music, and history.

He is interested in how people preserve knowledge, express creativity, and build communities through technology and culture.

Much of his curiosity comes from the belief that there is always something new to discover, understand, or learn.
