Hello, I'm Axel.

I live in Mexico, I'm 48 years old and I'm a nerd dad to a teenager and a soon-to-be teenager. I'm into all sort of things: drawing, illustration, lettering, music (vinyl, cassette tapes, cd, digital), movies, tv, cooking.

I have worked in roles related to design and front-end development for almost 30 years. I lived through the browser wars, the web standards movement, social media becoming mainstream, social media crashing down, and the slow decay of privacy online.

Today I'm very much into the IndieWeb, the Small Web, and the Fediverse.
