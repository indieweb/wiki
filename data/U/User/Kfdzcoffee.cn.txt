=== Who am I? ===

A high school senior born in 2009, ENFP (though I often bounce between E and I – that’s so me).

The online name “Coffee Bean” (咖啡豆子coffee) came about quite casually. I once had a kitten with brownish fur, and since I couldn’t think of a good name at the time, I called it Coffee Bean.

Back then, I was still young and had just discovered Douyin (around 2020, if I remember correctly). I saw many pet bloggers sharing their lives with their pets, and I desperately wanted to be one of them.

So I changed my online handle to “Coffee Bean coffee” (since I found that “Coffee Bean” alone was already taken). But in the end, I never became a pet blogger.

Before that, I had a rather scary habit – I used my real name everywhere online. Every social platform displayed my full real name, until I added a stranger online and suddenly realized how unsafe that was – I had to change it immediately.

But I couldn’t think of a new name, so I just used the Douyin name, and I’ve kept it ever since. (I figure, “if you keep changing it, who knows who you are?”)

==About my site==

===[https://blog.kfdzcoffee.cn/ Domestic Site]===

This is the fifth version of my little site.

 '''Version 1''' – The most technically challenging one for me (not that I have much tech skill anyway). Built with [https://hexo.io/zh-cn/ Hexo]. It was the one with the most pitfalls – back then, errors popped up at almost every step (seems like most tutorials assume all environments are already set up), but I managed to get it working.

 '''Version 2''' – Built with [https://valaxy.site/ Valaxy]. This is when I bought my current domain – `kfdzcoffee.cn` – named just as simply. This old version is still preserved here for nostalgia: [https://oldblog.kfdzcoffee.cn/ Old blog].

 '''Version 3''' – Switched to Halo. This was when I finally got a VPS (mainly because I couldn’t stand not being able to update from anywhere – static sites were too cumbersome, and I wanted to tinker with other fun stuff). This version was rather unstable and often crashed.

 '''Version 4''' – Moved to the famous WordPress with a premium theme (ouch, my wallet! but it looked gorgeous). The reason for the switch was that I wanted a more minimalist site, so I cut many features.

 '''Version 5''' – The one you see now. Back to Halo, because I love its plugins.

====[https://intblog.kfdzcoffee.cn/ International Site]====

This is the third iteration of my international site, and it’s been a bumpy ride.

 '''Version 1''' – Built with WordPress, but the server specs were too weak, so I couldn’t maintain it and it was left idle.

 '''Version 2''' – Built with Valaxy. Updates were more frequent, but since it’s static, updating was still inconvenient, so I always wanted to switch back to a dynamic system.

 '''Version 3''' – The current one, powered by Typecho. I spent a lot of time tweaking the theme in the admin panel to make most visible content English-friendly for international visitors.
