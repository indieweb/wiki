== About ==
[https://someguy.us/ SomeGuy.us] is my personal blog. I've kept a personal site in one form or another since the mid-nineties, when publishing on your own domain felt like a small act rather than a marketing exercise. It still feels that way to me.

Most of what I post is short: poems, small reflections, and occasional notes from the corners of software I still like to tinker in. I'm working toward writing more about scripture — not sermon transcripts, but the smaller studies that come out of preparing to preach: a passage that opened up, a word worth sitting with, a connection I hadn't seen before.

The rest of my week I spend as a CPA running [https://killencpa.com/ Killen & Associates], the firm my father started decades ago. Before that I was a software developer and web designer, which is why this site exists at all. I preach on Sundays at the [https://edistochurch.org/ Edisto Island Church of Christ].

== Topics ==
Topics I write about on [https://someguy.us/ my blog], drawn from the site's current tag set:
* Jesus, prayer, supplication, veneration, redemption, renewal, awakening, sin, slavery, accountability, morning, light, humor, haiku, fair

Longer-term I expect the mix to lean further toward scripture study notes alongside the poetry and short reflections.

== my indie web setup ==
* [https://someguy.us/ https://someguy.us/] is my primary home, where I post:
** short poems, haiku, and reflections as [[articles]]
** occasional scripture study notes as [[articles]]
* The site publishes an [[Atom]] feed for [[subscribe]]rs and includes [https://someguy.us/blogroll/ a blogroll].
* Fediverse creator attribution ([[rel-me]]-style linkage back to my [[Mastodon]] account) is set in page metadata so replies and shares on the fediverse can be traced back to me.

=== authoring practices ===
Posts are authored as [[Markdown]] (with some [[Nunjucks]] templates for structural pages) in [[Visual Studio Code]] and committed to [https://github.com/scottkillen/someguy.us GitHub]. See the [[#colophon|colophon]] below for the full build stack.
