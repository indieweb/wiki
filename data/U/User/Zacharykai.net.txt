== Intro ==
Hi! I'm Zachary Kai, a space fantasy writer, offbeat queer, twenty-something, curiosity enthusiast, traveler, zinester, and avowed generalist. I'm Australian, and oscillate between living there and overseas (right now: Kosovo).

I started [https://zacharykai.net my site] in 2024, when I moved from publishing as Leo Flynn to writing under my own name, and have loved tinkering with it ever since.

My sparkline and chat nickname: {{zachary.kai}}

=== Setup ===
* Hand-typed [[HTML]] and [[CSS]] in VSCodium, transferred over SFTP, hosted on an Ubuntu VPS. See my [https://zacharykai.net/colophon colophon].
* A handful of PHP scripts for automations, plus a self-hosted [[FreshRSS]] instance for myself and my sites.
* [[Public domain]] ([[CC0]]), no analytics, scripts, tracking, or cookies.
* Source: [https://github.com/zacharykai/personal github.com/zacharykai/personal]
