'''S David Prince''' (also known as '''SDavidPrince''') is a Nigerian law student, writer (freelance journalist), artist, and web tinkerer interested in the [[IndieWeb]], personal websites, and independent publishing.

I write, I draw, I code. My website, [https://sdavidprince.com sdavidprince.com], is my personal space on the web, where I share my work, explore my interests, and experiment with making the web more personal.

== Projects and interests ==

* '''Personal web:''' [[IndieWeb]], WordPress, personal websites, and digital identity.
* '''Writing:''' Journalism, essays, legal research, and digital publishing.
* '''Law and technology:''' Technology law, intellectual property, privacy, and digital governance.
* '''Art and comics:''' Drawing, visual storytelling, and independent creative projects.
* '''Web development:''' HTML, CSS, JS, PHP, XML, Blogger and WordPress themes, plugins, block patterns, and IndieWeb integrations.
