== About ==
I'm Joshua Morris. My personal home on the web is [https://joshuamorris.info/ joshuamorris.info] — a notebook, workbench, and directory for notes, reading, projects, experiments, and open-web identity. I write about software and practical building at [https://methodicalfunction.com/authors/joshua-morris/ Methodical Function]. I also run [https://joshternet.org/ Joshternet], a directory of Joshes on the independent web.

Interests wander between the digital and physical: the open web, field computing, amateur radio ([https://www.qrz.com/db/KN6NUF KN6NUF]), overlanding, camping, hardware, and science.
