=== Share Buttons ===
* [[ShareOpenly]] is a single button, and can be expanded to different locations
* There is research that "share buttons" have very poor click rate
** Fediverse sharing requires an extra step because of the different instances, whereas for Twitter, it's just one single URL
** Indiekit has a <code>/share</code> endpoint that can be added to make use of ShareOpenly
*** Fields: URL param, content, and name
*** https://shareopenly.org/integrate/
* {{artlung}} uses it on Smorgasborg - e.g. https://smorgasborg.artlung.com/feed-patterns/
** URL to share to looks like https://shareopenly.org/share/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fsmorgasborg.artlung.com%2Ffeed-patterns&text=“Feed+Patterns+for+URLs+for+a+few+sites”+
** Cookie is on ShareOpenly site and not on the site featuring the button
*** Correct ^ cookie set on ShareOpenly. No third party lib added to the sharing site. Just a link formatted as above with url and text parameters
* {{aciccarello}} added a share button and the rel=share-url to his site  integrating with Indiekit, but still trying toget the content to appear
** https://github.com/aciccarello/ciccarello.me/commit/0e047cd1324efc1568e7f0e7b6b8807f8ffda3fd

=== Create Day ===
* What is [[Create Day]]?
** A day where you say hi at the beginning of the day, do a short intro of what you plan to do, and at the end of the day, share with the group a demo of what you did
** Usually held on a day where people are more likely to be off / free from work
** It's flexible, free to jump in and out (co-working format), promotes information sharing and helping each other!
** [[Create_Day]]
*** Probably first event Naty attended, [[events/2025-12-25-create-day]]
* David is interested in organizing one to have an excuse to work on his site

==== HTML in the Park zine/card from Toronto's HTML Day event ====
* HTML Energy's IG account shared a photo of a card with a list of prompt/ideas for making a HTML
** Credit goes to original post on Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/p/DbqSAa2tNK7/
** A reposted link by Tabitha: https://archive.tabitha.computer/?Y2026-m08_Aug

# Make a site that makes you feel calm.
# Make a site the wildlife in this park would want to read. What's a good website for a worm? What's a fun webpage for a sparrow?
# Embody the spirit of a food product of your choosing.
# Make a site that's a letter to a friend. Is there an envelope? How do you sign off a website?
# What's your favourite place in Toronto? Make a website like a historical plaque, telling people about what you think is special about it.
# Find a lesser used HTML tag what uncovered beauty hides within its brackets? (suggestions:
<blink>, <map>, <bdi>. ‹ruby>)
# Make a website that breaks out of the square box of the browser window. Or perhaps, uses the window as a window?
# How can a website move like the wind?
# Design a site that feels like taking a long walk through a park.
# Think of a fun childhood memory. Make a website that's a shrine to that moment.
