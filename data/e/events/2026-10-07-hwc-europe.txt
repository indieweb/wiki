== Notes ==

New guidance for demos:
* Focus on one thing you want to share
* Share something you think will be interesting to the group (ideally something others can use)
* Keep your demo under 2 mins long
* Focus more on what you made (rather than the tech details); people interested in the tech side can always ask after the demo.

* Angelo has been posting RSVPs to the IndieWeb events page for a while
** Previous UX said "In reply to [url]" and below that "RSVP yes to ..."
** Now says "I'm going to [event name]", where [event name] is linked.

** h-entry contains u-in-reply-to h-event. Event microformats are pulled from the event page itself, like dt-start and dt-end.

** Summary: presentation of post is improved, and has richer microformats.
