=== Q: Why did blogging go away for younger people? ===
* It is easier to post to social media, low barriers to entry
** {{artlung}}'s visualization shows how [[Instagram]] became ascendant, and also Twitter
** But it is more rewarding, and you have more control, to post to your own blog
* Thomas has similar answers
** Technorati was a blog search engine: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technorati
** There was a notion of a blogosphere—people who wrote on blogs
*** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blogosphere
*** Julie & Julia film: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julie_&_Julia
* What are blogs? A serial capture tool—what was done on what date
** Can be private, fiction, family blogs, etc
** Blogs are just a way to organize writing, or media (photos, videos, artwork)
* The rise of Snapchat—short video that disappears in 24h
** The disappearance of content, no ability to search for or archive those memories
** Blogs can be vulnerable—some people may not want to put something on there that can be tracked, or archived
*** Whereas, longevity to posts is important to some
** Posting to your own site means the data is controlled by you, not relying on third-party companies that may close down or losing access to your account due to different reasons (violating terms)
** Silos that offer export tools is always appreciated as it allows the user to move to different tools, e.g., Neocities
** Tech stack changes are common, e.g., Wordpress to Ghost

=== Q: Challenges of encouraging the younger generation to join the IndieWeb? ===
* It may feel like a lot of work building something compared to using a pre-built service with a community, e.g., Instagram, Tiktok
* Educatating the reasons content ownership
* Easy to get attracted by existing friends on silos and the addiction of doom scrolling
** The ability to have cross references to things in the past, ability to search for old connections is doable on your own site if content was primarily posted on one's site or imported at a later stage
* Popular services may seem too big to fail, but they can disappear capriciously
** [[site-deaths]]
* [[POSSE]] and  [[PESOS]] are concepts that Lex came across while discovering IndieWeb
** Coming across tools like Micro.blog, Neocities
** [[Neocities]] provides a low-effort way of starting out quickly, and supports a full export of all the files you build on their platform, promoting content ownership
