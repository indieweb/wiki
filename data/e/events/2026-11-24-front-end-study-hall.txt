* CSS random
** What can it be used for?
*** Carnival last year: one host wished submissions were ordered randomly. Now that could be implemented with CSS. CSS has an "order" property. That is an actual application of random that serves a need of someone in the community that is practical.
*** There are maybe game-like applications; things animate, things are staggered in various ways; little rotations -- these kinds of things are fun.
** Do you need to use a polyfill?
*** Can you use @supports to check if random is available, otherwise a default value could be set?
** Could you have a list of ten hidden images, and use random() to unhide one?
*** It may not be possible to use random() inside a selector.
*** Could you set an animation delay that is 10 years away, and decide one item doesn't have a delay using random()?
*** Use z-index? (more intuitive than order)
*** There could be more ways to do it too.
*** The DOM HTML source order is still intact with random().
** If you use loading=lazy, the image should not show until it is needed.
*** We'll need to experiment to see what the effects on the network would be if you have 10 images -- would they all load? Is there an implementation that means only one would load?
