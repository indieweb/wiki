== Notes ==

[[File:hwc-nuremberg-2026-07-08.jpg|thumb|alt=The participants of HWC Nuremberg 2026/07 (the "usual" style)|The participants of HWC Nuremberg 2026/07 (the "usual" style)]]

{{tilman}} shared that he finished the web feedroll he originally started working on at [https://indieweb.org/2026/Nuremberg IndieWebCamp Nuremberg]. The project can be viewed at https://tilman.me/feedroll. He explained that it uses [[OPMN]] in the background, which allows subscriptions to be downloaded, though he noted that the alignment on the site is still a bit off.

{{bjoern}} announced that he is now using fixed width Monaspace fonts for his website, drawing inspiration from {{paultibbetts}} site. He mentioned that he might add a heading line to his main page with links to specific sections and noted that some layout improvements are still needed using media queries. Additionally, he discussed his [[digital garden]] workflow in relation to Obsidian Publish.

{{jkphl}} demonstrated Apparat, the publishing platform he is currently building, which utilizes concepts similar to [[Obsidian]]. It features [[YAML]] frontmatter for notes without a strict schema and includes a CLI that he previously demoed at [https://indieweb.org/2026/D%C3%BCsseldorf IndieWebCamp Düsseldorf]. During this discussion, it came up that a CLI for Obsidian exists as well. Joschi showcased the Apparat web interface, which includes a text field, the ability to suggest links, and the creation of relations that have specific attributes rather than just simple links. He mentioned that a proper markdown editor is in the works. Apparat also features a chat mode powered by a local LLM utilizing semantic search, which offers the option to create new records and notes directly from the generated answers.

Una discussed exploring her thought process and how she intends to utilize the Apparat platform. She is currently trying to find ways to digitally document her real-life sensory experiences, such as touch and sound.

Dimitry, who is a C/C# programmer by trade, shared that he has set up [[Apache]] and [[PHP]] as he prepares to build a new website. He has been looking into various frameworks and is trying to avoid heavy dependencies, noting that the options he is currently considering include [[React]], [[Astro]], and [[NextJS]].
