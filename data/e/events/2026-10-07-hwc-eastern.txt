=== Webmentions & Comments ===
* Webmention are linked mentions on your site (source) that you want to notify to the target
** Used to be called [[Trackback]]
** It can be private (you see the webmentions in a RSS feed) or it can be displayed on the frontend of your site with https://webmention.io/#show-mentions
* Thomas use WordPress for one of his site, WP has Webmention plugin which is automated, https://wordpress.org/plugins/webmention/
** Other site has a custom CMS which doesn't post webmentions
* Spam issues with WordPress / personal sites
** Mike blocked comments on his WP, and use webmention instead
** Thomas used [[Akismet]] and is pretty good for catching spam comments
** Naty uses static forms for people to reply with anti-spam integration; tried Cloudflare Turnstile or BotPoison to good effect
* Q: Webmention protocol - Does it support hashtags? What's the best way to integrate Ghost site? How to login with your site?
* https://indiekey.id use your website to authenticate, add some lines in <code>head</code>
