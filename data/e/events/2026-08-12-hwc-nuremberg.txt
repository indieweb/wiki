Thanks to {{tilman}} for hosting us at the [https://d.th-nuernberg.de/en/ design faculty of Technische Hochschule Nürnberg Georg Simon Ohm], even if he wasn't present this time.

See also the matching entry on the [https://homebrew-website.club/t/homebrew-website-club-august-12th-2026/ HWC Nuremberg Discourse].

== Notes ==

[[File:8120004.jpg|thumb|alt=The participants of HWC Nuremberg 08/2026|The participants of HWC Nuremberg 08/2026]]

{{bjoern}} shared that he completed some minor fixes to his website's search functionality. He directed the group to a [https://rakhim.org/honestly-undefined/19 comic] regarding blogging and also discussed [[concerts]], highlighting [https://concerts.lynnandtonic.com/ Lynn's concert page] as a particularly cool implementation.

Max focused on accessibility improvements for his site, specifically adding folding capabilities to codeblocks and adjusting color contrast. He also updated his image gallery, finalizing the work he originally started at [https://indieweb.org/2026/Nuremberg IWC Nuremberg]. The gallery now features new, configurable layout options for images and functions seamlessly on mobile devices.

Wolfgang utilized the [https://www.screamingfrog.co.uk/seo-spider/ Screaming Frog] tool to scan his website and identify underlying issues. He asked the group for advice on whether to optimize the parameters returned by a curl check; however, the consensus was that no optimization was necessary. Additionally, he made adjustments to his site's navigation.

Thomas initiated a discussion regarding image rights when linking to external websites and utilizing their images. Although no lawyers were present :), the group agreed that proper usage generally depends on the specific image license. Proposed ideas included taking a screenshot of the target website to use instead, and the group emphasized that one should always include the image source.

Una showcased image cards she created for her songs, which function as an elaborate message and comment board, noting that she also printed some of these cards physically on seed paper. She presented an interactive visualization featuring a path from beginning to end that website visitors can freely draw on, as well as a project for the song *suka duka* featuring a timeline and lyrics. This sparked an idea to add song timestamps to the dots on the left side of the interface. Furthermore, Una shared her newly created portfolio page and discussed her ongoing experiments with integrating soundscapes. In response, Max directed Una to [https://mynoise.net mynoise.net], shared examples of soundscapes he had built for a student project, and recommended a [https://blog.scottlogic.com/2020/10/09/ergo-rabbit-hole.html Scott Logic blog post] as an excellent example of how to link text and audio effectively.
