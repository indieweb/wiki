Homebrew Website Club Nürnberg was an IndieWeb meetup IRL that took place on 2026-10-14. The last event before this one was held on [[events/2026-09-09-hwc-nuremberg|2026-09-09]].

Thanks to {{tilman}} for hosting us at the [https://d.th-nuernberg.de/en/ design faculty of Technische Hochschule Nürnberg Georg Simon Ohm].

See also the matching entry on the [https://homebrew-website.club/t/homebrew-website-club-14-oktober-2026/ HWC Nuremberg Discourse].

== Notes ==

[[File:hwc-nuremberg-2026-10.jpg|thumb|alt=The participants of HWC Nuremberg 10/2026|The participants of HWC Nuremberg 10/2026]]


{{cowglow}} is launching a journal on his MC Scrpt website to write more frequently, while also configuring a trick-or-treat map and implementing magic links for user invitations. 

We welcomed Thomas to the group with a round of introductions, where he shared his transition from building [[Blogger]]-based websites for his sister-in-law to offline JavaScript development, including an app he designed to visually communicate his eye health issues to his doctor. Thomas is now heavily exploring [[AI]], impressively running a custom inference engine on a 16-year-old Windows 7 PC, alongside developing an artificial life Boids simulation, an EPUB reader, and custom forum software.

Eugen shared insights from his W3Schools Responsive Web Design course, while {{maxhaesslein}} discussed a color profile bug in his photo gallery that was causing shifted colors and clipped highlights. {{maxhaesslein}} resolved this by migrating his image processing tooling from [[GD]] to [[ImageMagick]], a fix he also rolled out across several new mh.gallery releases, and demonstrated his automated camera metadata extraction. 

Finally, Wolfgang shared his experiments with Lightbox 2, leading to a group discussion on his date-handling code and a recommendation to upgrade to the much-improved Lightbox 3.
