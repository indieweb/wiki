* Why should you use :root?
** It's useful for managing colours. You can keep them all in one place.
** You can reference your colour variables and other properties anywhere instead of duplicating them.
** Consider naming things by action / use (i.e. disclaimer box, main colour) instead of by colour.
** Fonts: base, alt, mono, serif -- in future, it can be changed at root and will change globally instead of having to change the font everywhere.
** WP uses many base colours in CSS variables at the :root
