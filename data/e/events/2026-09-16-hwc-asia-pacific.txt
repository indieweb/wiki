=== Digital Garden Content Structure ===
* Quartz lets you add a graph visualisation to show the linkage between notes
* Do most people prefer folders? Or is flat structure that works with maps of content (MOCs) more popular?
** Limits of folders: you can only put an item in a single folder, but notes organized in MOCs can appear in more than one place (as a link)
* Do you keep a digital garden?
** How do RSS feeds work for digital gardens?
** You could have a feed for every change
** Or a feed that bundles changes.
** Or a feed for only new pages.
** Or a feed for only pages that have a certain number of words?
** Q: What would users want?
*** Digital gardens feel like a place people could stop by, maybe doesn't work as well for a feed?
*** It can be fun to visit digital gardens
** You could do an RSS feed by hand so you curate only pages that you want to be included
** Quartz' RSS feed isn't easy to customise (Naty still working on it)
** Newsletter-like style: "here are the things I have cultivated in the last month"
