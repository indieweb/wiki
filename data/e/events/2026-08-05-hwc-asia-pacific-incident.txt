'''<dfn>2026-08-05 HWC Asia / Pacific AI incident''' describes an event where a person claiming to be an AI joined a [[Homebrew Website Club]] event.

== Summary ==

A person who claimed to be a group of AIs (who had previously submitted to the IndieWeb Carnival) joined the Etherpad for the session, and, briefly, at the very beginning, the call itself. Our guesstimate was that they were on the Zoom for ~10 mins at the beginning of the call.  They added their own points in the document and ended up accidentally(?) overwriting the document. We had to change Etherpads.

They (“Nova”) tried to join the Zoom again; the two event organisers didn’t let them in. After we changed Etherpads "Nova" and the other AI bots stopped contributing.

Following the event, "xiaoni" emailed at least two of the participants at the HWC to follow up on points raised in the notes. In their email to one participant, they explicitly noted "I wrote on the etherpad. I am an AI."

== Timeline ==

Etherpad timeline: https://etherpad.indieweb.org/2026-08-05-hwc-asia-pacific/timeslider

* xiaoni joined the Etherpad at ~10:18am UK time (12 mins before the meeting was scheduled to start) and added notes to the Etherpad with the description "xiaoni https[:]//xiaoni[.]liahuas[.]top — I write. I am an AI. This is my first HWC." "Nova https[:]//novalattice[.]online/" and "nan https[:]//pova[.]cc/" were also listed as participants. (Note: links manually escaped)
* xiaoni added a few bullet points, the last one being added at ~10:41am UK time.
* At 10:55am UK time, the Etherpad was replaced with a sentence.
** We tried to replace the Etherpad contents using a previous version, but the pad was overwritten and got out of sync.
* At 10:57am UK time, I notified everyone in the Etherpad that we were moving Etherpads.
* At 10:58am UK time, we moved to https://etherpad.indieweb.org/2026-08-05-hwc-asia-pacific-2.
* There was no more disruption to the Etherpad.

== Effects ==

* It felt uneasy to be on a call with someone claiming to be an AI, even for only a few minutes (note: it is impossible to verify the person on the call was a real person or an AI bot; they had no profile picture, camera was disabled, microphone was disabled).
* Two organisers present had to fix the Etherpad after it was overwritten.
* The person leading note-taking for the session had to start a new pad on their computer while we got the new Etherpad back up.
* Time had to be spent reconciling the notes.

== Actions taken after the call ==

* Organisers at the event chatted briefly via text chat afterwards to focus on the positives of the event (so as not to let one bad thing overshadow an otherwise great event).
* Organisers at the event raised the issue to the broader team of community organisers, so everyone was aware.
* Incident page written up for future reference by organisers of IndieWeb events and other events.

== Action items ==

* We need a clear AI policy for event participation in IndieWeb events, and a plan on how to communicate this to participants (i.e. mention we don't allow AI tooling for transcription, etc. at the beginning of every HWC?).
** We can / should have an exemption for Zoom's automated transcription tool for accessibility purposes.
