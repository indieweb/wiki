== WHAT TO DO ON A CREATE DAY ==
* start a new blog post!
* Start a new slash page! improve an existing page! Upload a photo and write about it! Write a tribute about something that's your favorite! read a page on the https://indieweb.org wiki and make edits! Check your site against http://indiewebify.me ... and have fun!
* 100 things you can do on your personal website: https://jamesg.blog/2024/02/19/personal-website-ideas
* Feel free to ask questions. The best creative outcomes come from effort and a plan!
