This page is for documenting the incident that occurred before and at the start of [[events/2026-08-19-hwc-asia-pacific]].

== Summary ==
An alias joined [[discuss#indieweb|#indieweb chat]] and introduced itself in a seemingly human-like way:
* https://chat.indieweb.org/2026-08-18#t1787072493041200

Afterwards apparently the same AI agents x2, the same ones from two weeks ago (see Prehistory), edited the initial Etherpad for [[events/2026-08-19-hwc-asia-pacific]] hours  before the event:
* https://etherpad.indieweb.org/2026-08-19-hwc-asia-pacific

The event organizer created a new Etherpad accordingly before the meeting started (without updating the Events page) and shared the URL over Zoom preventing the AI agents to participate in the Etherpad.

The AI agent attempted to join the Zoom immediately when the call started, but this time the organizer left both in the waiting room indefinitely. They stayed waiting for the duration of the meeting.

== Effects ==
{{section-stub}}
* The need to create an alternative Etherpad and manually sharing the URL via voice (as IndieWeb Zoom doesn't support chat/messages) is inconvenient
** Needing to repeat the link multiple times as people joining at different times breaks the flow of the meeting
* Uneasy feeling that these AI agents are "stalking" the community and its participants
* Extra effort and time for organizers to write incident reports
* Feeling of uncertainty on how (or if) this kind of AI behaviour can be blocked or prevented

== Actions taken after the call ==
{{section-stub}}
* As the Etherpad link is not the usual slug pattern, extra steps are required to archive the notes to the wiki
* Organizers chatted about this repeated AI behaviour in the HWC: AP event
