=== Q: Is this retro tracker (using bloom filters) privacy friendly? ===
** https://88x31.lol/
* Bloom filters - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloom_filter
** Fast scaling but not unique, less bits (but accurate enough)
** Cannot reverse check as it is approximate data
* Browser fingerprinting would be bad:
** eg time of visit, machine info, use JS to check what networks, extensions, ip address etc
** Creating a profile of the user, and then storing it
* SHA256 is unique and cannot reverse, expensive to store as everything is unique (large size)
* Thanks Ross for explaining how bloom filters work! —Naty
