Homebrew Website Club Nürnberg was an IndieWeb meetup IRL that took place on 2026-11-11. The last meeting took place on [[events/2026-10-14-hwc-nuremberg|2026-10-14]].

Thanks to {{tilman}} for hosting us at the [https://d.th-nuernberg.de/en/ design faculty of Technische Hochschule Nürnberg Georg Simon Ohm].

See also the matching entry on the [https://homebrew-website.club/t/homebrew-website-club-11-november-2026/ HWC Nuremberg Discourse].

== Notes ==

[[File:hwc-nuremberg-2026-11.jpg|thumb|alt=The participants of HWC Nürnberg 11/2026|The participants of HWC Nürnberg 11/2026]]

Stefan Sommer joined us for the first time, hi! After a round of introductions, we talked about his history in web development and the different outlets he has on the web.

{{bjoern}} showed his new interface for creating HWC meeting notes. It's a streamlined interface where you can add participants, have a shared cooperative notepad and can add links and photos. In the end, {{bjoern}} can export the notes in different target formats and automatically post in on the different platforms (if they support it). There we some requests for improvement, though:

* fix selection for the notes section (for now you can only select it by clicking on the active lines)
* make the textbox resizable
* prevent accidental checkout of participants (especially if they are already mentioned in the notes) 

Because {{bjoern}} vibecoded the platform, we talked a bit about vibecoding, AI agents and the implications of using them.

{{bjoern}} also found the TIL page from Michael Heap and got inspired by his homepage, so he redesigned his own homepage. He also explained how he added multiple columns in his Markdown/Obsidian workflow.

{{cowglow}} showed his open source Halloween map and the history behind it (that goes back until 2019). He successfully used it during this years Halloween.

Aleksandr Trutanov created a new website for his friend, who creates the Hard Hit Magazine, with stunning visual effects. He also added a little metal quiz game for the 404 page that makes finding a 404 much more enjoying. Also, if you finish the quiz flawlessly, you find a special, hidden page.
In another project, Aleksandr Trutanov made a PWA for his book reading list.

Wolfgang Gruner showed some features he uses on his WebEdition website as Stefan Sommer also uses the software. Later on, Stefan Sommer showed his WebEdition instance that is a central point for all his web presences, a little CSS 3D experiment for a "Winkekatze" (that we promply used as a background for our photo) and a website he made to show some new HTML5 features that now require less JS on your webpage.

The IndieWeb Camp Düsseldorf on April 17th and 18th seems to be somewhat confirmed, so expect to see a few of us there (and at the Beyond Tellerrand conference afterwards).

In the end {{maxhaesslein}} told about his RSS feed fix. His feed files were cached and readers that respected the caching time didn´t refresh the RSS for a month. Also, he now uses the upload time for the picture date so his workflow doesn´t mess up the RSS entry order.
