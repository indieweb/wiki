Homebrew Website Club Nürnberg was an IndieWeb meetup IRL that took place on 2026-09-09. The last event before this one was held on [[events/2026-08-12-hwc-nuremberg|2026-08-12]].

Thanks to {{tilman}} for hosting us at the [https://d.th-nuernberg.de/en/ design faculty of Technische Hochschule Nürnberg Georg Simon Ohm].

See also the matching entry on the [https://homebrew-website.club/t/homebrew-website-club-september-9th-2026/ HWC Nuremberg Discourse].

== Notes ==

<!-- [[File:XXXXXXX.jpg|thumb|The participants of HWC Nuremberg 09/2026]] -->

{{tilman}} shared that he has been more actively micro-blogging over the summer, publishing both short notes and longer posts on [https://knot.tilman.me knot.tilman.me]. In August he also published his first research paper, [https://tilman.me/garden/Publishing+on+the+web Publishing on the web]. It is deep investigation on the options creative practitioners have today to publish to the web. 
The text is too long to read together during the club, but any kind of feedback is very welcome!

Max showed a work in progress of a new website project that makes heavy use of glitch effects. The group discussed how he built the flickering typography with CSS-animated text-shadows – lots of small details and very nice layout and typography throughout. He also presented ''Klangteppich'', a tool for ambient soundscapes for tabletop roleplaying games, which he 100% vibecoded as a test project using DeepSeek V4 Flash via [https://opencode.ai Opencode.ai]. He wants to find out how well AI-assisted code will hold up in the long term.

Una showed the progress on her website [https://thedriftinghealer.com The Drifting Healer], which has gained several new pages. Among them is an interactive sound room in which visitors can layer the instruments she uses – bowls, rain stick, drum and others. The group was thoroughly impressed and gave feedback.

Wolfgang brought a problem with his photo gallery: his users want direct links from one photo to the next and the previous one. This brought up some discussion about his options. He might look into [https://getkirby.com Kirby] for the future.
