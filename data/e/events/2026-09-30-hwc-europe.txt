=== New guidance for demos: ===
* Focus on one thing you want to share
* Share something you think will be interesting to the group (ideally something others can use)
* Keep your demo under 2 mins long
* Focus more on what you made (rather than the tech details); people interested in the tech side can always ask after the demo.

** Dave is looking to implement a middleware for his site that will look for .env, .php, etc. and add the corresponding IPs to a list, and another middleware that will reject requests for 24 hours from IPs that have been added to the list.

* Dave: Q on Mastodon: do you prefer RSS, Atom, JSON? (majority was RSS, 0 said JSON)
** https://toot.wales/@mort8088/117303445705015889
** Why is RSS the default feed? Is it because we always refer to feeds as RSS? Many sites say they offer RSS even though it is Atom.
** RSS was first. When people switched to Atom, everyone knew what RSS was, and that was their frame of reference.
** Is there a point in supplying RSS, Atom, and/or JSON, or should you stick with one?
** Most feed readers will pick up RSS and Atom; not everything picks up JSON.
* https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don%27t_repeat_yourself
** Better to maintain one thing that works
** James recommends publishing an h-feed that converts to RSS
*** https://jamesg.blog/2024/06/06/publish-h-feed
*** Having a visible RSS link as a means to introduce people to RSS who may otherwise be unaware of it
*** https://subscribeopenly.net/subscribe/?url=https://jamesg.blog/feeds/posts.xml
*** Orange feed icon is still recognisable to some
*** You may want to have a /feeds page that links to your feeds
*** https://vanderwal.net/vdwalxml.php has example of local link, link to Subscribe Openly, and also a link to the feed validator from the W3C.
