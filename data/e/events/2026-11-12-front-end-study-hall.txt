== scroll-snap ==
* https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Guides/Scroll_snap
* Building walkthroughs and tooltips to indicate to someone that an action is visible.
* Users need to know that an affordance exists before they can use it.
* If there is no visual indicator, users might not know something exists.
* There are global interace patterns, like swiping, but equally someone needs to know a pattern applies.
* The idea of making web apps first class in terms of their behaviour on mobile devices.
* Scroll snap lets you add more native-feeling scrolling effects.
* https://playground.jamesg.blog/scrollsnap-experiment.html
** How could you have a three-panel interface that lets you focus on the middle element first?
