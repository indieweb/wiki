== Challenges ==
[[blogging challenge]]s for the month of September:
* [https://smallweb.dreamwidth.org/13580.html Small Web September] is an annual month-long event organized on [[Dreamwidth]] that's dedicated to working on small web projects. The event was first launched in [https://smallweb.dreamwidth.org/2335.html 2024] and continued in [https://smallweb.dreamwidth.org/tag/small+web+september 2025].
* [https://xixxii.neocities.org/projects/suckatsomethingseptember Suck at Something September] “suck at something september is a month-long art jam for making something that sucks! (particularly web pages, but you can do other stuff if you want to.)”
* [https://tantek.com/2026/244/t1/september-blogging-challenge-wassup Web And Standards September Useful Posts] (#WASSUP) — post 30 times during September about useful things and tips for the web and open web standards
